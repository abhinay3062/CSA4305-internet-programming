Online Doctor Appointment Booking System

Welcome to MedCare Hospital

MedCare Hospital provides an online appointment booking platform that allows patients to search hospitals and doctors, check available appointment slots and book appointments according to their preferred date and time.

System Features

  • Patient Registration and Login
  • Search Hospitals by Location
  • Search Doctors by Specialty
  • View Doctor Details and Ratings
  • Check Appointment Availability
  • Book and Cancel Appointments
  • Patient Check-in Form
  • Appointment History
  • Doctor Reviews and Ratings
  • Hospital Location / Map
  • Admin Appointment Management
  • Hospital Management
